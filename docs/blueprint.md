# **App Name**: CuraAI: Your Medical AI Companion

## Core Features:

- AI-Powered Medical Learning: Allows medical students to ask medical questions. Uses Gepard AI to research reliable sources and provide simple, step-by-step explanations.
- Smart Image Analysis: Enables users to upload images of medical questions or medicine boxes. AI extracts text or identifies the medicine and provides structured answers, formulas, and usage information.
- Humanized AI Doctor Consultation: Offers a human-like AI consultation experience where users can discuss symptoms and medical queries. The AI Doctor asks follow-up questions and provides treatment suggestions and advice.
- Medical Product Marketplace: Provides a verified marketplace where pharmacies and medical businesses can list medical products and tools. Includes a seller registration system with document verification and AI-based product categorization.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft blue (#77B5FE), representing trust, health, and intelligence. It conveys a sense of calm and professionalism, crucial in the medical field.
- Background color: Very light blue (#F0F8FF), providing a clean and clinical feel, promoting readability and focus.
- Accent color: Pale violet (#D0BFFF) to highlight key actions and information. This analogous color provides visual interest without overwhelming the user.
- Headline font: 'Poppins' (sans-serif) for a modern, clean, and easily readable style.
- Body font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for clear and accessible information presentation.
- Use clean, minimalist icons related to health, medicine, and AI. Icons should be consistent in style and color to maintain a professional look.
- Maintain a clean and intuitive layout with clear sections for learning, consultation, smart analysis, and marketplace. Use white space effectively to avoid clutter.
- Incorporate subtle animations for transitions and loading states to provide a smooth user experience. Animations should be professional and not distracting.
